Description

Aloe, cucumber and green tea deliver cool, translucent clarity inspired by smooth sea glass.

Ingredients & Materials

• Soy wax • Coconut wax • Cotton wick • Aloe, cucumber & green tea fragrance oil

Care Instructions

• Trim wick to 5mm before each burn • Allow a full melt pool to prevent tunnelling • Burn for 2–3 hours at a time • Keep away from drafts, curtains, and flammable objects • Always place on a heat‑safe, level surface • Do not burn for more than 4 hours • Never leave a candle unattended • Extinguish fully before sleeping • Keep out of reach of children and pets
