Our Story

Inspired by the calm beauty of the British coastline

The Beginning

Coast & Co London began with a simple idea — to capture the quiet clarity of shoreline mornings and bring that feeling into everyday spaces. Mist‑blue horizons, driftwood textures, salt‑white simplicity — these became the foundation of our aesthetic and the heart of our brand.

Our Philosophy

We believe in slow living, natural materials, and thoughtful design. Every product is crafted to feel soft, calm, and grounding. From hand‑poured candles to woven textiles and frosted glassware, our collection reflects the colours and textures of the coast.

Materials That Matter

We work with trusted UK suppliers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability. Natural linen, soft wool, hand‑finished ceramics, frosted glass, soy and coconut wax — each material is chosen with intention. Learn more about our sourcing through our product catalogue.

Designed for Calm

Coast & Co is more than a brand — it’s a mood. A breath of calm. A moment of stillness. Our products are made to soften your space, brighten your mornings, and bring a sense of coastal clarity to your everyday rituals.

A Note from Our Founder

“I created Coast & Co London to share the feeling I get when I’m near the water — calm, clear, grounded. Every product in this collection is designed to bring that same feeling into your home.”

— Harry, Founder
