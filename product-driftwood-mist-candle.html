Driftwood Mist Candle

£24.00
EAN · 5012345600029

Warm driftwood, amber and soft smoke inspired by foggy shorelines.

Ingredients & Materials

  • Soy wax
  • Coconut wax
  • Cotton wick
  • Driftwood & amber fragrance oil

Care Instructions

  • Trim wick to 5mm before each burn
  • Allow a full melt pool to prevent tunnelling
  • Burn for 2–3 hours at a time
  • Keep away from drafts, curtains, and flammable objects
  • Always place on a heat-safe, level surface
  • Do not burn for more than 4 hours
  • Never leave a candle unattended
  • Extinguish fully before sleeping
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets
